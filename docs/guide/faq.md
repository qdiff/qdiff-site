# Frequently asked questions

### Is qDiff free?

Yes. Every comparison, every view and every export works without paying. The
free version only shows a small “Unlicensed” mark in the tab bar. A one-time
purchase removes it and includes future premium extras. There is no
subscription and no advertising.

### Does the purchase unlock any features?

No — all features are already available. The purchase removes the mark. If we
add premium extras later, they join the same one-time purchase rather than a
new product.

### How do I restore my purchase?

Open **Settings → Premium** and choose **Restore Purchases**. Purchases are
tied to your Apple ID, so they work on your other Macs too.

### Which Macs are supported?

macOS 12 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. See
[system requirements](./download.md#system-requirements).

### Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. qDiff works offline, needs no sign-in, and has no background service.
It opens only the files and folders you choose.

### Can it compare whole folders and archives?

Yes. Folders open as a tree with status filters and copy / sync actions.
A .zip or .tar archive can be compared like a folder without unpacking.

### What about pictures and non-text files?

Pictures can be viewed side by side or as an overlay. Files that are not
plain text can be compared down to the smallest detail. See
[more ways to compare](./more.md).

### Will you upload or analyse my files?

No. Nothing is collected, tracked or synced. The full explanation is in the
[Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

### Is there a Chinese interface?

Yes, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and more, with light
and dark appearance.

### Something is not working. What should I do?

[Contact us](./support.md) with your macOS version and a short description of
what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
