# Meet qDiff

qDiff answers one question: **what changed?** Put two versions next to each
other and everything different is marked in colour, so you can stop comparing
line by line with your eyes.

<img src="/screenshots/files.svg" alt="Two files compared side by side" class="guide-shot" />

## Three things people use it for

- **Two files** — a draft and its revision, a config before and after, two
  exports from the same tool. Changed words are highlighted inside the line.
- **Two folders** — a working folder and its backup. New, changed and missing
  files are easy to pick out, and you can bring the two sides back in sync.
- **A project folder** — everything you touched, gathered in one list. Read
  each change and commit without switching windows.

Pictures, compressed archives and files that are not plain text are welcome
too. [See more ways to compare](./more.md).

## Start in three steps

1. Open qDiff and drag two files or folders onto the window — or choose them
   from the File menu.
2. Look at the coloured lines. Click the small map on the side, or use the
   previous / next buttons to walk through every change.
3. Edit, copy or sync what needs fixing. Save a picture of the comparison if
   someone else should see it.

::: tip No setup needed
There is no account to create and nothing to configure before the first
comparison. Everything works offline.
:::

Every comparison is available for free. The free version only shows a small
