# Contact us

Something confusing, a file that will not open, an idea for the next version —
we read every message.

**Email:** [qdiff@w3cub.com](mailto:qdiff@w3cub.com)

You can write in English or Chinese.

## What helps us help you

- Your macOS version and which Mac you are using.
- The qDiff version (open **About qDiff** from the app menu).
- What you were comparing, and what you expected to see.
- A screenshot, if the problem is visible on screen.

Please do not attach files you would rather not share — a description or a
cropped screenshot is usually enough.

## Before writing

The [FAQ](./faq.md) answers the most common questions, and the
[introduction](./introduction.md) walks through the first comparison. If your
question is about privacy, the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) spells out exactly
what qDiff does and does not read.

## About purchases and refunds

Purchases and refunds are handled by the Mac App Store and your Apple ID.
For billing questions, Apple Support is the quickest route; for restoring a
purchase inside qDiff, see the [FAQ](./faq.md).
