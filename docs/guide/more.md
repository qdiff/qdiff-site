# More ways to compare

Text is only part of the story. qDiff opens several other kinds of content
with the same side-by-side idea.

<img src="/screenshots/more.svg" alt="Two pictures compared side by side" class="guide-shot" />

## Pictures

Put two images next to each other to compare them comfortably, or overlay
them to see exactly where they differ — handy for design revisions and
almost-identical photos.

## Compressed archives

A .zip or .tar archive can be opened directly and compared like a folder.
No unpacking, no temporary files to clean up afterwards.

## Files that are not plain text

When two files look identical but behave differently, qDiff can compare them
down to the smallest detail, including differences a text view cannot show.

## Save a comparison as a picture

Select a change and export it as a clean, two-column image — ready to paste
into a message, a document or a review. The picture has no markings added.

## Make it yours

- **Appearance** — light and dark, following your Mac or set manually.
- **Language** — English, Simplified & Traditional Chinese, Japanese, German, Spanish and French.
- **Fonts and shortcuts** — adjust reading comfort and use the keyboard for
  the actions you repeat most.
- **Recent comparisons** — reopen something you looked at yesterday from the
  welcome screen.

Everything above is included in the free version.
