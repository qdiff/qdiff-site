# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

**The short version: qDiff does not collect personal data.** It is a local
tool. Your files stay on your Mac.

## What qDiff reads

qDiff opens only the files and folders you choose in the app, and processes
them on your device to show the comparison. If you grant access to a folder,
that access is kept for your convenience and can be removed in Settings at
any time. Nothing is uploaded to us.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect names, email addresses or usage statistics.
- We do not track you across apps or websites.
- We do not require an account or a sign-in.
- We do not show advertising or include analytics or third-party tracking.
- We do not sync or back up your files to any server.

## Network access

qDiff works offline. The only optional network request is the in-app “More
Apps” page, which loads a public list of apps. That request contains no
information about you, your files or your device, and no usage data is sent
back.

## Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is handled entirely by the Mac App Store and
your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. You can restore a purchase
from Settings inside the app.

## This website

This site is a set of static pages. It has no login, no analytics and no
advertising cookies. Opening a page does not require you to give us anything.

## Children

qDiff is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information
from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
Any change that affects what the app reads will also be described in the app
itself.
