Get & install ​

qDiff is a small, native Mac app. Install it and your first comparison is two drags away.

Free to use, with an optional one-time purchase. No account, no subscription.

Install in a moment ​

  1. Open the Mac App Store page and click Get, then Install.
  2. Launch qDiff from the Applications folder or Launchpad.
  3. Drag two files — or two folders — onto the window. That is the whole setup.

Granting access to a folder

The first time you compare a folder, macOS may ask whether qDiff may look inside it. Choose Allow. You stay in control: only folders you pick are read, and you can change this later in Settings.

Free, with an optional one-time purchase ​

Every comparison, every view and every export works without paying. The free version simply shows an “Unlicensed” mark in the tab bar. A single purchase removes that mark and includes future premium extras — no subscription, no ads, no account. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored any time from Settings.
