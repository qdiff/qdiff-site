Good to know

Quick answers before you download

Is qDiff free?
Yes. Every comparison works without paying. The free version only shows an “Unlicensed” mark in the tab bar; a one-time purchase removes it and includes any future premium extras. There is no subscription.
Which Macs are supported?
macOS 12 or later, on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. qDiff works offline and only opens the files you choose. Nothing is uploaded.
Can it compare whole folders?
Yes. You get a tree with new, changed and missing files, status filters, and copy or sync actions right in the list.
Is there a Chinese interface?
Yes — Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and many other languages, with light and dark appearance.
What happens to my files?
They stay on your Mac. We do not collect, track or sync them. See the Privacy Policy for the full story.
